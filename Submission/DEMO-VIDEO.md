# Demo video shot list (attach to App Review)

Record on a real iPhone Duo, tabletop posture, screen recorder or a second
camera. 60–90 s, no narration required (add captions).

1. Home → Quick Play; consent card shown once, then round starts (5 s).
2. Tabletop: fold to tabletop, show the lid (word on the flat colour + card) and
   the outer display facing the guessers, countdown colour migrating green → amber → red (15 s).
3. Blind taps: Correct / Skip on the flat half with score feedback (10 s).
4. Outer display: scoreboard mirroring score + countdown, REC indicator (10 s).
5. Mid-round revocation of the outer display: game carries on (5 s).
6. Round summary → Game Over → reaction reel playback at 1×/2×, Save to Photos (20 s).
7. Play Without It path: same game, no reel, no outer display (10 s).

`0922 test.mov` in the repo root is an earlier test recording (untracked); not
suitable as the final demo.
