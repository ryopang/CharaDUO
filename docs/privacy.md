# CharaDUO Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 29 September 2026_

**CharaDUO does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.**

## What the app does with your camera, microphone and photos

- **Camera and microphone.** During a live round, CharaDUO can record the guessing
  team's reactions (video) and the table's sound (audio) so the players can watch a
  short highlight "reaction reel" when the match ends.
- **Everything stays on your device.** Video and audio are written to a temporary
  folder on your phone. They are never uploaded, sent over a network, analysed or
  transcribed.
- **Automatic deletion.** Anything you do not save is deleted when you leave the match,
  and the temporary folder is cleared again every time the app launches.
- **Photos.** The app asks for permission to add to your photo library only when you tap
  **Save** on a reel. Nothing is saved unless you choose to.
- **You are in control.** You can play without the camera or microphone at all
  ("Play Without It", or deny the permissions), and Settings has switches for the
  reaction camera and for sound. The whole game works without them.

## Other data

- No accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party
  SDKs.
- The app itself makes no connections to any server.
- Game settings (for example your language and sound preferences) are kept in the app's
  local storage on your device and never leave it.

## In-app purchases

The first 10 games are free. CharaDUO then offers optional in-app purchases ("10 More
Games" and "Unlimited Games").

- Purchases are handled entirely by **Apple's App Store**. CharaDUO never sees or stores
  your payment details or Apple ID, and we receive no information about you from a
  purchase.
- To keep your game count and purchases across a reinstall, the app stores a small
  counter (games played, packs bought, unlimited unlocked) in your device's **Keychain**.
  It stays on your device.
- Use **Restore Purchases** (on the purchase screen and in Settings) to restore an
  Unlimited unlock on a new device. Bought "10 More Games" packs are not restorable
  across devices, because Apple treats them as consumable.

## Children

CharaDUO collects no information from anyone, including children, and contains no
advertising or links to other apps or websites.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
